Your presentations. More control.

Nullaconexio Docs is made for people who value simple tools and want more control over how they work. It can be opened in a browser, and the standalone HTML file can be kept locally for an offline workflow.

  • ✓No cloud account required
    Open the editor directly in your browser.
  • ✓Designed without telemetry
    Focused on your work, not unnecessary tracking.
  • ✓Keep a local copy
    Download the HTML file and store it wherever you like.
Offline tip

Take the editor with you.

Download App.html and open it from your device in a compatible browser. For offline use, images should be saved locally and inserted from local files; remote image links may not load without internet access.
